I am a mathematician and researcher specializing in classical and modern plane and projective geometry, specifically the algebraic and synthetic structure of $n$-point and $n$-line configurations, conic envelopes, projective involutions, and invariant geometric systems.

I obtained my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics and Telecommunications from Télécom ParisTech (Paris, France), advised by Prof. Laurent Decreusefond and Prof. Philippe Martins, focusing on stochastic spatial models, point processes, and dimensioning of cellular networks.

6-Point Configurations & Conics

Investigating the projective and affine properties of 6 general points ($6P$), including the polar envelope conic $\mathcal{E}_Q$, the vertex involution $\mathcal{J}$, canonical equilateral hyperbolas, and cubic Cremona transformations.

Structure of complete quadrangles ($QA$) and complete quadrilaterals ($QL$), including isogonal conjugacy, orthology, Miquel configurations, and invariant circles such as the Tulip circle and pedal reflection circles.

The Vertex Involution and Canonical Conics of General Six-Point Configurations

Vu Thanh Tung • October 2026 • 12 pages with figures & proofs

For any six general points $A_1, \dots, A_6$ in the projective plane, consider the six 5-point conics $\mathcal{C}_i$ passing through all points except $A_i$. For any point $Q$, their polar lines $\ell_i$ are proven to touch a single envelope conic $\mathcal{E}_Q$. When $Q$ is evaluated at a vertex $A_i$, the envelope conic degenerates into a pair of lines meeting at a point $A_i'$. We prove that the correspondence $A_i \mapsto A_i'$ is an involution on the configuration, and determine its explicit algebraic representation and connections to classical cubic Cremona transformations.

Geometry Gallery

Visualizations and computational figures from my research on configurations, conics, and quadrangles.

The 6-Point Vertex Involution $\mathcal{J}$

Given 6 general points $A_1, \dots, A_6$, the five-point conics $\mathcal{C}_i$ and their polar lines envelope conics $\mathcal{E}_Q$. For vertices $A_i$, the envelope degenerates into pairs of lines whose intersections define the involution partner $A_i' = \mathcal{J}(A_i)$ and associated canonical hyperbolas.

Equilateral Rotation & Perspective Conics

Geometric transformations generated by equilateral rotations, perspective triangle centers, and the envelopes of perspective axes across family parameters.

Equilateral Quadrangle Family (EQF)

Loci of centers, inscribed circles, and dual envelopes in equilateral quadrangle systems, displaying symmetry groups and invariant pencil behavior.

Isogonal Conjugacy in Quadrangles

Study of quadratic transformations and orthology in complete quadrangles, relating isogonal conjugates to nine-point conics and pedal configurations.

Miquel-Morley Incentre Configuration

The interplay between complete quadrilateral in-circles, Miquel points, and Morley equilateral triangles, demonstrating remarkable concurrent centers.
