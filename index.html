Dr. Vu Thanh Tung

Senior Quantitative Researcher • Mathematician • Music Artist
Olympiad Research (2020–present) WorldQuant (2015–2020) École Polytechnique Télécom Paris Paris 6 (Probabilités Appliquées) Geometry & Pure Math Music: Tung Tulip Vietnam

I am a Quantitative Researcher working in quantitative finance for global hedge funds since 2015 (based in Vietnam) — currently at Olympiad Research (ex Keebeck Alpha) from 2020 to present, and previously at WorldQuant from 2015 to 2020. My professional expertise includes quantitative Alpha generation, statistical arbitrage, stochastic point processes, and portfolio combination algorithms.

I am a graduate of École Polytechnique (Palaiseau, France) where I double majored in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science, followed by an Engineering Degree and Ph.D. from Télécom Paris and a Master 2 in Applied Probability from Université Paris 6 (Pierre et Marie Curie). Prior to quantitative finance, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Orange Labs in Paris.

Outside finance, I pursue two creative passions: pure mathematical geometry (researching orthologic & perspective systems and simplexes, published in The American Mathematical Monthly, The Mathematical Gazette, and Forum Geometricorum) and music, producing and singing tracks for creative enjoyment under the artist moniker Tung Tulip.

Mathematical Research Interests

Independent research in classical & modern geometry and stochastic models.

Δ

Perspective & Orthologic Triangles

Investigating orthologic and parallelogic triangles, generalizations of Droz-Farny's line theorem, and perspective centers in classical triangle geometry.

Droz-Farny theorem Orthologic triangles Parallelogic systems Perspective centers
⊗

Higher-Dimensional Simplexes

Extending classical orthology from triangles in the plane to median-orthologic simplexes in arbitrary dimension $\mathbb{R}^n$, utilizing vector and barycentric methods.

Simplexes in $\mathbb{R}^n$ Median-orthology Centroidal properties
∂

Stochastic Geometry & Spatial Models

Point processes, Poisson Voronoi tessellations, spatial modeling of cellular access networks, and analytical outage and handover probability calculations.

Poisson point processes Spatial modeling Cellular networks Stochastic optimization
