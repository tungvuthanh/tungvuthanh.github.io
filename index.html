Dr. Vu Thanh Tung

Quantitative Researcher • Mathematician • Music Artist
Quantitative Researcher @ Hedge Funds École Polytechnique Télécom Paris Paris 6 (Probabilités Appliquées) Geometry & Pure Math Music: Tung Tulip Vietnam

I am a Quantitative Researcher working for hedge funds since 2015 (based in Vietnam). My professional focus centers on statistical modeling, point processes, and algorithmic strategies for financial markets.

I am an alumnus of École Polytechnique (Palaiseau, France) with double majors in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science, and earned my Engineering degree and Ph.D. from Télécom Paris, alongside a Master 2 in Applied Probability from Université Paris 6 (Pierre et Marie Curie). After completing my doctorate, I worked as a research engineer at Orange Labs before transitioning to quantitative finance.

Beyond quantitative finance, my two greatest creative passions are pure mathematical geometry (researching perspective/orthologic systems, simplexes, and configurations published in journals such as The American Mathematical Monthly and The Mathematical Gazette) and music, which I compose and release for leisure under the artist moniker Tung Tulip.

Mathematical Research Interests

Independent research in pure geometry and stochastic models.

Δ

Perspective & Orthologic Systems

Investigating orthologic and parallelogic triangles, generalizations of Droz-Farny's line theorem, and perspective centers in classical triangle geometry.

Droz-Farny theorem Orthologic triangles Parallelogic systems Perspective centers
⊗

Higher-Dimensional Simplexes

Extending classical orthology from triangles in the plane to median-orthologic simplexes in arbitrary dimension $\mathbb{R}^n$, utilizing vector and barycentric frameworks.

Simplexes in $\mathbb{R}^n$ Median-orthology Centroidal properties
∂

Stochastic Geometry & Spatial Models

Point processes, Poisson Voronoi tessellations, spatial modeling of cellular access networks, and analytical outage and handover probability calculations.

Poisson point processes Spatial modeling Cellular networks Stochastic optimization
